Estancias & Glaciers

Eight days through Chilean Patagonia, from the Strait of Magellan to the lakes and open grasslands of Torres del Paine.

The route

Day 1

Punta Arenas

Begin on the Strait of Magellan. The first evening leaves time to settle in and meet your guide.

Days 2–3

Puerto Natales

Travel north across the steppe. A seasonal stop at Magdalena Island and a sailing up Last Hope Sound toward the Balmaceda and Serrano glaciers are part of the proposed route.

Days 4–6

An estancia near Torres del Paine

Visit the Milodón Cave area and settle into a ranch for three nights. One day looks outward to the park and its lakes. Another stays close to the estancia, with time to explore the steppe.

Days 7–8

Return to Puerto Natales

Spend a final night in town before your onward flight.

This is a sample itinerary for planning. Sailing depends on the weather. Dates, lodging, walking options and availability are being confirmed.
