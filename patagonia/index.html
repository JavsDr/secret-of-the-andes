Walking & comfort

Will this pace suit me?

Look at day 3 first. Balmaceda is visible from the boat. Seeing Serrano up close involves going ashore and walking almost 2 km (1.2 mi) round trip, including steps and footbridges. This is the longest scheduled outing on the proposed route.

If one of you would rather sit out the walk, tell us before booking. We’ll check where you can wait, whether there’s a seat and how you would rejoin your companion.

From the strait to the steppe

The eight-day route

Download the itinerary (PDF) ↓

These are the proposed overnight stops. Locations, room access and timings are confirmed in your personal itinerary.

  1. Day 1

    Arrive in Punta Arenas

    Settle in on the Strait of Magellan. Keep the evening free after your flight.

    Getting around Airport-to-hotel drive followed by check-in. No scheduled walk. If stairs matter, we’ll verify the path from the vehicle to your room.

    Sleep in Punta Arenas

  2. Day 2

    Cross the steppe to Puerto Natales

    Travel north by road. In penguin season you can also request a morning sailing to Isla Magdalena, with a landing and an island trail.

    Time on the move The scheduled bus takes about 3 hr 15 min, a reference for the road journey. The optional island excursion adds about 4½ hours in total and operates October–March, weather permitting. Ask for the road-only version for a shorter day.

    Sleep in Puerto Natales · First of two nights

  3. Day 3

    Sail to the Balmaceda and Serrano glaciers

    Leave Puerto Bories by boat. About 2½ hours up the fjord, Balmaceda comes into view. At Puerto Toro, the forest trail follows the shore to the Serrano viewpoint.

    Boat and trail Allow a full day. The viewpoint walk is almost 2 km (1.2 mi) return, with steps and footbridges. CONAF describes an adapted first 250 m (820 ft). Boarding assistance, a place to wait, and any weather alternative must be confirmed for your sailing.

    Sleep in Puerto Natales · Same room planned

  4. Day 4

    Milodón Cave and the estancia

    Visit the cave where remains of the extinct giant ground sloth were found, then continue by road to the estancia, a working ranch in the park region.

    At the cave CONAF lists the Cueva Grande trail at 350 m (0.2 mi) and about 30 minutes. The park describes wheelchair access with assistance to the interior viewpoint. We’ll check current conditions, assistance and the onward driving time for your ranch.

    Sleep at the estancia · First of three nights

  5. Day 5

    The lakes of Torres del Paine

    Go out by vehicle to see the mountains across the park’s lakes. The proposed stops are roadside viewpoints, with time to get out and take in the landscape.

    At each stop Expect to get in and out of the vehicle. Driving time and walking distances depend on the estancia and viewpoints. Tell us your comfortable walking distance so we can check the surface, steps and seating at each stop.

    Sleep at the estancia

  6. Day 6

    A day at the ranch

    Stay at the same estancia. Leave room for a morning on the steppe or an afternoon watching the weather move across the grassland.

    Your choice of pace No scheduled long drive. We can request a short outing on the property or a day at the lodge. Available paths, gradients and places to sit depend on the ranch selected.

    Sleep at the estancia

  7. Day 7

    Back to the waterfront in Puerto Natales

    Return across the steppe for a final night in town. Leave the afternoon open for the waterfront or a rest before the journey home.

    Return journey The day centres on the drive from the estancia. The waterfront stroll is optional. Your proposal will specify the transfer time and any planned breaks.

    Sleep in Puerto Natales

  8. Day 8

    Depart from Puerto Natales

    Allow the morning for check-out and your onward connection.

    To the airport No scheduled walk beyond hotel and airport access. Confirm your departure airport and transfer time with us before you book flights. Punta Arenas is a separate road journey.

Before you book

How your trip is priced

Send your month, number of travellers and room preference. Javier Droguett handles trip enquiries and will prepare a written proposal once accommodation and excursions have been checked for your dates.

  • Dates and group. We plan for up to 12 guests. The proposal confirms your departure, party size, assigned guide and language, and which excursions use a shared boat.
  • Price per person. The quote specifies currency, room occupancy, taxes, included meals, transfers, park entries and excursions, plus any extras. Ask for the total and cancellation terms before paying a deposit.
  • Travelling solo. Request a single-room quote. We’ll check room availability, any single supplement and the total for one traveller.
