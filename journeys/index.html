Two other ways to see the park

Both routes are sample itineraries. Ask Javier for lodging, travel time, group size, guide language and a written per-person quote for the dates you have in mind.

9 days / 8 nights

Torres del Paine, Unhurried

Four nights near Lake Pehoé give you several days in Torres del Paine from the same base. The sample journey begins in Punta Arenas, with three nights near Puerto Bories before you enter the park.

See the day-by-day route
  1. Punta Arenas. Begin in Punta Arenas, with the first evening on the Strait of Magellan.
  2. Puerto Bories. Travel towards Puerto Natales and Puerto Bories. A seasonal island crossing is one possibility if conditions and schedules allow.
  3. The fjord. Consider a boat day up Last Hope Sound toward Balmaceda and Serrano.
  4. An estancia or a day by the water. Use the same base to explore the coast or ask about a nearby estancia visit.
  5. Lake Pehoé. Travel into Torres del Paine for the first of four proposed nights in the lake area.
  6. Grey Glacier or the lakes. A glacier outing or a day by the park’s lakes can be discussed when your dates are known.
  7. Wildlife on the steppe. A day with a wildlife guide near Laguna Amarga is being considered. Ask about terrain and options for traveling on foot or by vehicle.
  8. A day to choose. Return to a viewpoint, choose a walk or spend more time around the lake.
  9. Back towards Puerto Natales. Plan onward travel after the return from the park.

6 days / 5 nights

Private Patagonia

A proposed villa stay for your own party near the eastern edge of Torres del Paine. Spend one night in Punta Arenas and four near the park. Javier checks the villa, guide and outings available for your dates.

See the day-by-day route
  1. Punta Arenas. Arrive in Punta Arenas and spend the first evening in town.
  2. Towards the park. Travel north to the eastern side of Torres del Paine. The working idea is a four-night villa stay.
  3. Views of the towers. Discuss a day around the Paine massif, with time for viewpoints or a walk appropriate to your plans.
  4. Steppe and wildlife. Consider a guided day in the open country near the park. Wildlife sightings depend on the day.
  5. A day shaped around your interests. Choose what deserves more time. Ask the lodge about the outings offered during your dates.
  6. Return to Puerto Natales. Leave the park area for Puerto Natales and your onward connection.
